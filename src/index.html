Fundamental Properties
Sector
The broad industry group the company belongs to. Mixing sectors reduces single-industry risk and helps diversify your portfolio.
—
—
Market Cap
The total value of all outstanding shares. Large-cap companies are usually more stable; small-cap names can grow faster but are more volatile.
—
P/E Ratio
Price-to-earnings ratio: the stock price divided by annual earnings per share. Lower values can mean better value; higher values usually imply stronger growth expectations.

Dividend Yield
The annual dividend as a percentage of the share price. It shows the cash income you can expect from owning the stock, before price changes.

52-Week High
The highest share price reached in the last year. It helps show how much momentum the stock has had and how close it is to its recent peak.
—
52-Week Low
The lowest share price reached in the last year. It helps show the recent downside range and how much room the stock has to recover.
