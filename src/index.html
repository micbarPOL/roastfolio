Fundamental Properties
Sector
A sector is a broad area of the economy. Good diversification involves holding assets across multiple different sectors.
—
—
Market Cap
Total market value of a company's outstanding shares.

Good: Large-cap ($10B+) usually means stability and steady dividends.
Riskier: Small-cap ($250M-$2B) can offer high growth but higher volatility.
—
P/E Ratio
Price-to-Earnings Ratio. Measures current share price relative to its per-share earnings.

Good: A lower P/E (e.g. 10-15) often means the stock is undervalued or a solid value play.
Bad: A very high P/E can mean it's overvalued, though high-growth tech companies often naturally have high P/Es.
—

Dividend Yield
The ratio of a company's annual dividend compared to its share price.

Good: 2% to 6% is generally considered a solid, sustainable yield.
Bad: An exceptionally high yield (e.g. 10%+) can be a "dividend trap", signaling the market expects a dividend cut soon.
—
52-Week High
The highest price the stock has traded at in the last year. Stocks trading near their high often show strong market momentum.
—
52-Week Low
The lowest price the stock has traded at in the last year. Stocks trading near their low might be undervalued bargains, or they might be facing serious fundamental issues.
