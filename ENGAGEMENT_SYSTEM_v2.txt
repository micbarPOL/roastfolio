ROASTFOLIO — ENGAGEMENT SYSTEM DESIGN  (v2 — revised)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Target: small retail investors (hobbyists, DCA loyalists, passive ETF holders)
Personality: premium fintech × witty financial advisor who has seen things
Tone rule: roast the behavior, never the person
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Revision notes:
  - Contribution streak narrowed to DEPOSIT-only (buy ≠ new capital)
  - No-panic-sell streak removed (selling is a valid decision, not a failure)
  - Rebalancing streak removed (too opaque to explain)
  - Four new streaks added: Withdrawal-Free, Goal, Green Month, Benchmark
  - Check-in streak counts GPW trading days only (no weekends, no Polish holidays)
  - Check-in milestone 365 days → 252 trading days (one full trading year)
  - Contribution streak milestones capped at 60 months (10yr/20yr removed as unrealistic)
  - Added 2M + 5M PLN portfolio value milestones
  - Time-based milestones anchored to first investment date (not signup)
  - Total contributed milestones removed (deposit + buy double-counts capital)
  - Monthly turnover milestones added (section 3.6)

1. UX ARCHITECTURE — ENGAGEMENT LAYER OVERVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The engagement system sits OVER the existing app as a light layer.
It never blocks core functionality. It rewards presence and consistency.

Four surfaces where it lives:
  A) Dashboard card  — persistent summary of streak + XP + active badge
  B) Achievement drawer — full badge collection, unlocked + locked (grayed)
  C) Monthly Recap screen — auto-generated on first open of a new month
  D) Inline toast/commentary — contextual roast triggered by user actions

Data inputs the system reads:
  - Transaction log (deposit/withdrawal/buy/sell dates, amounts, types)
  - Portfolio snapshots (value over time, allocation breakdown)
  - Monthly goal setting (user-defined deposit target)
  - Retirement plan target (if configured)
  - Session events (login dates, panel opens)

No external servers required initially — all computed client-side from
existing data the app already holds. Backend can persist badge unlocks
and streak counters to DynamoDB as a phase 2.

2. STREAK SYSTEM
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

──────────────────────────────────────────────────
2.1  Streak types
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

CONTRIBUTION STREAK  (primary — most meaningful)
  Definition:  consecutive calendar months with at least one DEPOSIT transaction.
               Buy transactions do not count — buying with existing cash is not
               new capital entering the portfolio.
  Grace period: 3 days into the new month (a deposit on Jan 2 still saves a Dec streak)
  Unit: months
  Milestones: 3 / 6 / 12 / 24 / 36 / 60

  Copy examples:
    3 months  → "Month 3. You're officially no longer in the experimental phase."
    6 months  → "Half a year of consistency. You've outlasted most New Year's resolutions."
    12 months → "One full year of monthly deposits. The market crashed at least once. You kept going."
    24 months → "Two years. This is no longer a phase."
    36 months → "Three years of monthly deposits. The habit is the investment at this point."
    60 months → "Five years. Statistically, you're fine."


CHECK-IN STREAK  (secondary — habit signal)
  Definition:  consecutive GPW trading days the user opens the app and views the dashboard.
               Only days when the Warsaw Stock Exchange (GPW) holds a session count —
               weekends and Polish public holidays are skipped entirely.
               A Saturday open doesn't advance the streak. A Friday miss breaks it.
               This keeps the streak meaningful: it tracks presence on days when
               the market is actually moving and decisions matter.
  Grace period: 24h buffer from last session open (not midnight-strict)
  Unit: trading days
  Milestones: 7 / 30 / 90 / 180 / 252
              (252 ≈ one full trading year on GPW — replaces the raw 365-day milestone)

  GPW non-trading days excluded:
    Weekends (Saturday + Sunday)
    New Year's Day (Jan 1)
    Epiphany (Jan 6)
    Easter Monday (moveable)
    Labour Day (May 1)
    Constitution Day (May 3)
    Corpus Christi (moveable)
    Assumption (Aug 15)
    All Saints' Day (Nov 1)
    Independence Day (Nov 11)
    Christmas Eve (Dec 24, half-day — treated as non-trading)
    Christmas Day (Dec 25)
    Boxing Day (Dec 26)
    New Year's Eve (Dec 31, half-day — treated as non-trading)

  Copy examples:
    7 days   → "Seven trading days in a row. Every day the market opened, so did you."
    30 days  → "Thirty trading days. That's six full weeks of showing up."
    90 days  → "A quarter of the trading year. The portfolio has been supervised."
    252 days → "A full trading year, every single session. The market opened 252 times. You were there."


WITHDRAWAL-FREE STREAK  (capital preservation signal)
  Definition:  consecutive calendar months without any WITHDRAWAL transaction.
               The goal is simple: money in, nothing out.
               Sells are not counted — reinvesting proceeds is a valid decision.
  Grace period: none (a withdrawal is a withdrawal)
  Unit: months
  Milestones: 3 / 6 / 12 / 24 / 36 / 60

  Copy examples:
    3 months  → "Three months without touching it. The compounding thanks you."
    6 months  → "Six months of leaving it alone. This is the discipline people talk about but rarely practice."
    12 months → "A full year without withdrawals. The portfolio is growing undisturbed."
    24 months → "Two years untouched. Whatever the reason, the number is bigger for it."
    36 months → "Three years. At this point the money has forgotten what a withdrawal even looks like."
    60 months → "Five years without a withdrawal. This is either impressive discipline or impressive forgetting."


GOAL STREAK  (commitment streak — most personal)
  Definition:  consecutive calendar months where the user deposits at least as much
               as their self-set monthly goal (configurable in settings).
               If the goal is updated, the streak continues — changing your ambition
               doesn't erase your track record.
  Unit: months
  Milestones: 3 / 6 / 12 / 24

  Notes:
    - Goal must be set to activate this streak
    - Goal can be as low as 1 PLN (no minimum enforced)
    - Suggested goal prompt appears after first deposit

  Copy examples:
    3 months  → "Goal hit three months in a row. The plan is becoming a practice."
    6 months  → "Six months of hitting your own target. You set the bar. You cleared it."
    12 months → "A full year of meeting your monthly goal. One of the more underrated things a person can do."
    24 months → "Two years of goal-consistent deposits. At this point the goal is too conservative. Raise it."


GREEN MONTH STREAK  (performance streak — partly luck, still meaningful)
  Definition:  consecutive calendar months where the portfolio value at month-end
               is higher than at month-start (before any new deposits, to isolate
               market performance from contribution effect).
  Unit: months
  Milestones: 3 / 6 / 12

  Notes:
    - Calculated on market performance only, not padded by deposits
    - Harder to sustain than contribution streaks — don't show as primary metric
    - Breaking this streak is never roasted — the market did it, not the user

  Copy examples:
    3 months → "Three green months in a row. The market cooperated."
    6 months → "Six months of positive performance. This is rare enough to notice."
    12 months → "A full year in the green, market-wise. Either great allocation or great timing. Probably both."

  Break copy:
    "Green streak ended. That's markets. The contribution streak still stands."


BEAT THE BENCHMARK STREAK  (quality streak — for engaged users)
  Definition:  consecutive calendar months where the user's portfolio total return
               exceeds the benchmark (user selects: WIG20, MSCI World, S&P 500).
               If no benchmark is selected, this streak is not available.
  Unit: months
  Milestones: 3 / 6 / 12

  Notes:
    - Only shown if user has benchmark comparison enabled
    - Genuinely hard to sustain — celebrated accordingly
    - Benchmark data pulled from the existing WIG/market data layer

  Copy examples:
    3 months → "Outperformed the benchmark three months running. Don't tell your index fund friends."
    6 months → "Six months beating the benchmark. Fund managers charge 2% for worse results."
    12 months → "A full year ahead of the benchmark. Statistically improbable. Practically impressive."

  Break copy:
    "The benchmark won this month. It happens. The important question is whether you still have a contribution streak."


──────────────────────────────────────────────────
2.2  Streak break UX
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

When a streak breaks, a toast appears — NOT a blocking modal.
Tone: wry acknowledgement, not shame.
One action offered: "Pick it back up" (opens deposit or relevant panel).

Example breaks:
  Contribution streak broken →
    "Contribution streak ended at {N} months. Life happens. The market continued without comment."

  Check-in streak broken →
    "You missed a trading session. The market opened without you. This time."

  Withdrawal-free streak broken →
    "Withdrawal streak ended at {N} months. The money had somewhere to be."

  Goal streak broken →
    "Missed the monthly goal. Happens. The streak counter is ready when you are."

The broken streak record is preserved as a personal best, displayed in the
achievement drawer as a "former streak" gray badge. You can beat your own record.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────
2.3  Streak freeze  (phase 2)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

One "freeze token" earned every 6 months of unbroken contribution streak.
Spent automatically if a month is nearly missed (within 3-day grace).
Display: small ice crystal icon next to streak counter when a freeze is available.

Copy: "Freeze available. Use it wisely. Or not. We're not your parents."

3. MILESTONES

Milestones are one-time events. They trigger a full-screen moment (brief,
skippable) and unlock a badge. Think: Duolingo achievement pop-up but darker.

  10 000 PLN   — "Five figures. A threshold that matters."

3.6  Monthly turnover milestones  (largest deposit in a single month)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Triggered by the single highest DEPOSIT month on record — i.e., the first time
  the user deposits above a threshold in one calendar month. Not cumulative.
  Rewards ambition in individual months, not just consistency.

  1 000 PLN in one month   → "First four-figure month. The habit is becoming an operation."
  2 500 PLN in one month   → "2,500 in a single month. Serious money, consistently deployed."
  5 000 PLN in one month   → "Five thousand in one month. The contribution is doing real work now."
  10 000 PLN in one month  → "Ten grand in a month. At this pace the portfolio notices."
  25 000 PLN in one month  → "A 25k month. Either a bonus, a windfall, or a lifestyle. Respect."
  50 000 PLN in one month  → "Fifty thousand in a single month. The math is changing."
  100 000 PLN in one month → "Six figures deployed in one month. You are no longer a casual investor."

  Note: this milestone tracks the personal best monthly deposit ever achieved.
  It unlocks once per threshold, on the month it is first crossed.

4. ACHIEVEMENT BADGE SYSTEM
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Visual design: small circular badges, dark backgrounds, single icon,
thin colored border by tier. Locked badges shown as silhouettes with
a hint of what they require. Collected in an "Achievements" drawer
accessible from the dashboard.

  ◻ Seed      (gray)      — participation / first steps
  🟤 Bronze    (amber)     — early consistency

  🟡 Gold      (yellow)    — genuine milestones
  💎 Diamond   (cyan/blue) — rare, long-term, legendary

5. PROGRESS TRACKING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

──────────────────────────────────────────────────
5.1  Investor Level  (XP system)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

XP is earned by real actions — not artificial engagement loops:

  Action                                 XP
  ─────────────────────────────────────────
  First login of the month               +10
  Any transaction recorded               +20
  Monthly deposit made (streak +1)       +50
  Monthly goal hit                       +30
  Milestone reached                     +100
  Badge unlocked                         +75
  Retirement plan updated                +30

Levels (named, not numbered):
  0 – 199      "Lurker"             — you opened the app. Good start.
  200 – 499    "Participant"        — something is happening here
  500 – 999    "Regular"           — showing up consistently
  1 000–2 499  "Contributor"       — money is moving
  2 500–4 999  "Operator"          — running a proper portfolio
  5 000–9 999  "Compounding"       — the math is doing more work than you
  10 000+      "Institutionalized" — you're in it now

Level displayed on dashboard as a small pill badge next to portfolio title.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────
5.2  "Next milestone" progress bar
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Dashboard shows a single progress bar to the nearest upcoming value milestone.
Label: "Next: 100 000 PLN — 73% there"
Sub-label: "At your average monthly deposit rate, ~14 months away"

This is always visible, always honest, occasionally dispiriting.
The dispiriting part is by design.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

12. DESIGN PRINCIPLES SUMMARY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Every metric ties to real behavior, not artificial engagement.
   XP is earned by depositing and hitting goals, not by opening the app
   repeatedly or completing tutorials.

2. Selling is a valid decision.
   No streak penalizes selling. No badge mocks sells. Withdrawals are
   tracked because they reduce compounding capital — not because they are wrong.

3. Roasts roast the pattern, never the person.
   "Sold at a loss" not "you made a bad decision."

4. Bad market moments are never roasted.
   Down 20%? The commentary system goes quiet. It returns when the portfolio does.

5. The tone is consistent with the app's premium fintech identity.
   No confetti. No level-up sounds. No cartoon characters.
   A minimalist Bloomberg that has read too much finance Twitter.

6. Retroactive fairness.
   Existing users get their history recognized on first launch.
   No one starts at zero because the feature shipped late.

7. Minimal intrusion.
   Every element can be dismissed, hidden, or collapsed.
   The engagement layer assists — it does not boss.

8. Honesty over dopamine.
   The progress bar to retirement shows the honest projection.
   If the math is bad, the app says so, gently, once. Then it moves on.

13. MULTI-PROFILE CALCULATION RULES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

13.1  MONTHLY DEPOSIT GOAL — ONE GLOBAL SETTING
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  There is exactly ONE monthly deposit goal per user — a single PLN
  value set in Settings → Achievements → Monthly goal.

  Calculation: total DEPOSIT transactions across ALL portfolios in a
  calendar month is compared against that single goal.

  Example: goal = 1 000 PLN. User deposits 400 PLN into IKE and
  700 PLN into XTB → total = 1 100 PLN → goal met → streak advances.

  Rationale: the goal tracks total capital deployment. Which portfolio
  receives the money is a portfolio-level decision unrelated to the
  monthly investment habit being rewarded.

  Changing the goal mid-streak does NOT reset the streak. The system
  records the habit (goal met Y/N), not the specific goal amount.


13.2  MULTIPLE RETIREMENT PLANS — PRIMARY PLAN SELECTION
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Users can create multiple retirement plans (e.g., conservative /
  aggressive scenarios). Exactly one plan drives achievement badges
  and the FIRE progress bar at a time.

  Selection rules (in priority order):
    1. The plan explicitly marked "primary" by the user.
    2. If none is marked: the most recently modified plan.
    3. A soft prompt in the achievements screen suggests marking one
       as primary when more than one plan exists.

  Switching primary plan:
    The FIRE progress percentage recalculates immediately against the
    new plan's target. No badge is revoked; no badge is re-earned.
    The retroactive history is simply viewed against a new target.


13.3  FIRE PROGRESS CALCULATION
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Formula:

    FIRE % = (sum of ALL portfolio current values) ÷ (primary plan
              retirement target) × 100

  All portfolios are included regardless of type (brokerage, IKE,
  IKZE, crypto). Rationale: retirement uses all available assets —
  arbitrarily excluding some portfolios would understate progress.

  Milestone thresholds: 10 / 25 / 50 / 75 / 90 / 100 %.
  Each threshold can only be crossed (badge awarded) once per plan.
  If the user changes primary plan, threshold crossings for the new
  plan are evaluated against their existing history independently.


13.4  IKE / IKZE UTILIZATION — ALWAYS PER-ACCOUNT
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  IKE and IKZE utilization badges and reminders are independent of
  the retirement plan system entirely.

  Calculation: sum of DEPOSIT transactions in the current calendar
  year into portfolios tagged "IKE" (or "IKZE") vs. the statutory
  annual limit (updated each year; hardcoded in the backend config).

  This works regardless of whether a retirement plan exists or is
  marked primary. It requires only that the user has a portfolio
  named or tagged as IKE or IKZE.

SECTION 14 — SARCASTIC COMMENTARY ENGINE  (roast-engine.js)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  "I'm not being sarcastic. I'm making accurate observations in a
   register that some people find socially uncomfortable.
   That is a them problem."  — tone calibration note, roast-engine.js

14.1  PURPOSE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  A contextual, rotating sarcastic commentary layer that surfaces
  one-line reactions to portfolio events. Tone: Sheldon Cooper —
  precise, condescending, accidentally supportive.

  Implemented in:  src/scripts/roast-engine.js
  Exposed as:      window.RoastEngine

14.2  CONTEXT OBJECT  (caller supplies these fields)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  changePct         number        — today's portfolio Δ% (signed)
  isATH             boolean       — portfolio at all-time high
  benchmarkDiff     number        — portfolio minus benchmark Δ% today
  depositGoalMet    boolean|null  — null = no goal set
  streak            number        — longest active streak (months)
  concentrationPct  number        — largest single-holding weight %
  concentrationName string        — name of that holding
  volatilityHigh    boolean       — elevated intraday swing detected
  swingPct          number        — intraday high/low range %

14.3  CATEGORIES  (14 total, 124 commentary entries)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Performance (auto-categorized from changePct):
    positive_mild        +0.1 % →  +1 %     10 entries
    positive_strong      +1 %  →  +3 %      10 entries
    positive_exceptional  > +3 %            10 entries
    negative_mild        −0.1 % → −1 %      10 entries
    negative_strong      −1 %  → −3 %       10 entries
    negative_severe       < −3 %            10 entries
    flat                  ±0.1 %             8 entries

  Context-triggered (override performance when condition is true):
    ath                  isATH = true        8 entries
    volatility_high      volatilityHigh      8 entries
    concentration_risk   conc ≥ 40 %         8 entries
    benchmark_beat       benchmarkDiff ≥ 1 %  8 entries
    benchmark_lag        benchmarkDiff ≤ −1 % 8 entries

  Lifecycle-triggered (call getForCategory() explicitly):
    missed_goal          month-end, goal unmet   8 entries
    strong_consistency   streak milestone events  8 entries

14.4  CATEGORIZATION PRIORITY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1. isATH                → ath
  2. volatilityHigh       → volatility_high
  3. concentrationPct ≥ 40 → concentration_risk
  4. benchmarkDiff ≥ 1    → benchmark_beat
  5. benchmarkDiff ≤ −1   → benchmark_lag
  6. changePct bands      → performance categories
  7. (default)            → flat

  missed_goal and strong_consistency bypass this chain entirely —
  they are triggered explicitly by lifecycle events, not daily.

14.5  ROTATION LOGIC
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Each category maintains a seen-index buffer of the last 3 picks.
  The engine draws from the pool excluding recently seen entries.
  When all entries have been seen, the buffer resets and rotation
  resumes from the full pool.

  This ensures no exact repeat within ~3 appearances of the same
  category, without requiring server-side state.

14.6  FRONTEND INTEGRATION
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Load roast-engine.js before any consumer script:

    <script src="scripts/roast-engine.js"></script>

  Daily dashboard commentary (example):

    const ctx = {
        changePct:         liveData.portfolioChangePct,
        isATH:             liveData.isATH,
        benchmarkDiff:     liveData.changePct - wigData.changePct,
        concentrationPct:  analytics.topHoldingWeight,
        concentrationName: analytics.topHoldingName,
        volatilityHigh:    liveData.swingPct > 3,
        swingPct:          liveData.swingPct,
    };
    const result = RoastEngine.getCommentary(ctx);
    roastBanner.textContent = result.text;
